Something is writing from the other side

It is 11:47 PM, October 31. The security operations center is empty except for you and the hum of the mail server.

Messages keep arriving from colleagues who never sent them, vendors with the wrong spelling, and an "IT desk" at addresses nobody owns. Some of tonight's mail is real. Some of it wants your password, your payroll, or your wire transfer.

  1. 1Inspect. Check the sender address, reply-to, links and attachments. Hover a link to see where it really goes.
  2. 2Decide. Mark each email Safe or Suspicious. Trust a phish or flag a real email and you lose a heart.
  3. 3Learn. Every answer reveals the red flags. Get three right in a row and the haunting gets harder.
